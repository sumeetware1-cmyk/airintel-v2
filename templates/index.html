DGCA Statutory Concession Registry: Hover badges for statutory guidelines ℹ️
Standard domestic tariffs without statutory quota concessions. Open to all travelers.
DGCA Student Statutory Quota: Applicable to bonafide students aged 12 to 26 enrolled in recognized institutions. Mandatory valid physical Student ID required at check-in. Grants +10 kg baggage allowance on domestic carriers.
Senior Citizen Concession: Applicable only to Indian citizens aged 60 years or above on the departure date. Government photo ID with DOB (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID) must be presented at the airline counter.
Defense Concession: Available to serving and retired military personnel of the Indian Armed Forces (Army, Navy, Air Force) and dependent family members. Official Armed Forces Service ID mandatory.
Healthcare Concession: Dedicated statutory discount for registered medical professionals (MBBS/MD) and nursing staff. Medical Council registration number or official hospital ID card required.
